Bonk Bow is a goofy 3D stickman archery duel for Android and the Solana Seeker. Pull back, aim, and bonk the other idiot before he bonks you.

Every duel is against the AI. Entry fees and shop items are paid in SOL (or SKR) from your own wallet through Mobile Wallet Adapter.
