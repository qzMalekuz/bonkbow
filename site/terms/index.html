The game

Bonk Bow is provided as is. We try to keep it working but make no guarantee that it will be available or free of bugs.

Payments

A duel costs an entry fee in SOL, shown before you pay. Shop items cost SOL or SKR. All payments go from your wallet to the Bonk Bow treasury on the Solana blockchain and are final: blockchain transactions cannot be reversed, and fees are not refunded for lost duels. You also pay the network's small transaction fee.

An entry fee that was paid but not used by a duel (for example, if the app closed) is kept as a credit for your next duel.

Shop items are cosmetic. They change how your stickman looks and do not help you win. They are unlocked on your device; you can restore them on a new install from the same wallet.

No prizes

Duels are against the computer. Winning pays in-game coins and experience only, which have no cash value and cannot be withdrawn.

Your wallet

You are responsible for your wallet and its keys. We cannot recover lost wallets or reverse payments sent by mistake.
